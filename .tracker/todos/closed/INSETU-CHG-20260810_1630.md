## Description
* **ExtensionContext Accessor Standardization (`bridge_vfs.py`, `cartographer.py`, `engine_gather.py`)**: Replaced direct `ExtensionContext` instantiations with `bp.get_context(workspace_id)` or direct `get_connection()` calls to comply with ADR 0017 and `EXTENSION_CONTEXT_ACCESSOR_MANDATE` linter rules.
* **Yomama Bridge Multi-Chunk Revert & TTL (`engine_bridge.py`)**: Updated `bridge_revert` to deserialize `chunks_json` and sequentially apply all constituent patch blocks during forward-replay rollbacks, attaching a 48-hour TTL (`ttl_expires_at`) to reverted ledger entries.
* **Gather Settings Schema Scoping (`engine_gather.py`)**: Annotated `max_context_size_kb` in `GATHER_SETTINGS_SCHEMA` with `"scope": "workspace"`.
* **Watchdog Native VFS Write Tracking & Trailing Debounce (`utils_core.py`)**: Integrated `_NATIVE_VFS_WRITES` tracking and a 10-second trailing timer (`_WATCHDOG_TIMERS`, `_WATCHDOG_DEBOUNCE_WINDOW`) in `FileSystemObserver` to suppress duplicate event bus firings and quiet external filesystem mutation storms.
