Abstract localized helper utilities and database architectures introduced during the V2 SDK graduation sweep into core, universally accessible frameworks to encourage ecosystem reuse and architectural integrity[cite: 1, 2].

* **Database Operations Abstraction:** Transitioned the localized query structures into a centralized, lightweight `DatabaseWrapper` exposed through `ctx.db` across all compliant extension substrates[cite: 1, 2].
