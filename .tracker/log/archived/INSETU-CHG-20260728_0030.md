## Description
* **Kernel Utility Bisect (ADR 0030)**: Bisected monolithic `utils_core.py` into Tier 1 Kernel utilities (`insetu/utils.py`) and Tier 2 Developer OS physics (`insetu/core/utils_core.py`). Refactored path resolution into Tier 1 `resolve_sandbox_path` and Tier 2 `resolve_logical_path`. Updated import targets across `app.py`, `auth.py`, `db.py`, `hooks.py`, `routes_fs.py`, `routes_system.py`, `vfs.py`, `workers.py`, and `sdk/extension.py`.
* **Secrets Vaulting in SDK Settings**: Upgraded `SettingsManager` in `insetu/sdk/extension.py` to route sensitive configuration keys marked `secure: True` to `secrets.json`. Added `secrets.json` and `*.env` to `insetu/defaults/gitignore.txt`.
* **VFS Save & Pre-File Save Hooks**: Decoupled repository initialization logic (`is_new_repo`) from `execute_vfs_save_physical` in `routes_fs.py` and implemented `@hooks.on('pre_file_save')` handler `handle_config_pre_save` in `routes_system.py`.
* **GatherStore Topology Decoupling**: Extracted repository configuration and topology states (`allRepos`, `targetConfigs`, `virtualContexts`) out of `AppStore` into `GatherStore`. Relocated `getFlattenedBuckets`, `packSelectionPayload`, and batch actions registration out of `app.js` into `gather.js`.
* **UI & Component Refactoring**: Refactored `InSetuFileModal` footer actions in `fs.js` to use `<insetu-async-btn>` and Lit reactive properties. Cleaned up obsolete task modal shortcuts in `app.js`.
* **Worker Ledger & Fitness Hardening**: Moved `sys_vfs_ledger_daemon` job registration out of `workers.py` to `engine_gather.py`. Updated fitness functions in `tests/fitness/rules_python.py` and `core.py` with strict tier isolation rules.
