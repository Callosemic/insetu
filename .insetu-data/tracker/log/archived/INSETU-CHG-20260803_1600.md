## Description
* **JobManager Background Coalescing & Deduplication (`insetu/kernel/extension.py`, `insetu/kernel/workers.py`)**: Enhanced `submit_immediate_job` and `JobManager.submit` with a `coalesce=False` parameter to deduplicate queued or active background tasks.
