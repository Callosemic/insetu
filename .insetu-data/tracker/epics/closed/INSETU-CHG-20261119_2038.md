## Description

* **Single-Row App Shell & Unified Global Gear Menu (`index.html`, `ui_system_settings.js`, `insetu_sdk.js`, `system.js`, `ui_filter_pills.js`):**
  * Added `#gear-pinned-slot` (`slot="gear-pinned"`) inside `<sutram-app-shell>` in `insetu/templates/index.html` immediately ahead of `<sutram-selection-tray>` and `<insetu-system-settings>`.
  * Refactored `InSetuSystemSettings` to render a declarative chip-row `<sutram-dropdown ?chipRows=${true}>` uniting `System`, `Repositories in scope`, `Workspace`, `Display Theme`, and `Viewport Grid`, retiring `menuOpen` and the manual `document` outside-click listener.
  * Extracted `toggleRepoInPinSet` (`window.inSetu.utils.toggleRepoInPinSet`) as the pure SSOT state transition for `'ALL'` vs. specific repository pin sets, shared across `InSetuSystemSettings` and `InSetuRepoFilter`.
  * Added `ensureDefaultWorkspaceLayout` in `insetu_sdk.js` and `system.js` to seed new workspaces (without saved layouts or explicit hash targets) with Column 1 (`Prompts` active + `Files`), Column 2 (`Gather`, focused, `mobileViewportIndex: 1`), and Column 3 (`Yomama`).
* **Global Repository Pin Uniqueness (`bridge_history.js`, `bridge_projections.js`, `bridge_store.js`, `bridge.js`, `gather.js`, `ext_git.js`, `ext_citations.js`):**
  * Removed duplicate per-view `<insetu-repo-filter>` toolbar dropdowns from `InSetuExtBridgeHistory`, `InSetuExtGather`, `InSetuExtGitDiffs`, and `InSetuExtCitations`, and retired `InSetuExtBridgeActions` (`headerActionComponent`) from `bridge_projections.js` and `bridge_store.js`.
  * Migrated `InSetuExtCitations` onto `this.ecosystem?.pinnedRepos` and moved its view-specific `showOrphansOnly` filter into declarative `menuActions` / `menuStores: ["Citations"]` on the `lib-main` sub-navigation slot.
* **Force-Refresh Parity, Gesture-Safe Clipboard & Selection Ownership (`insetu_element.js`, `fs_store.js`, `fs.js`, `fs_actions.js`, `gather_quickpack.js`, `ext_git.js`):**
  * Subscribed `InSetuElement` to both `'insetu:force-refresh'` and `'sutram:force-refresh'` so `<sutram-subtab-menu>`'s four-part quick-control bar refresh button triggers `onForceRefresh()`.
  * Added `copyTextFromPromise` (`fs_store.js` / `window.inSetu.vfs.copyTextFromPromise`) to initiate `ClipboardItem` promise writes synchronously inside user gestures across `fetchAndCopy`, `file-copy`, `file-copy-expanded`, and `gather-pack-copy`.
  * Updated `ensureFreshContext` (`fs.js`) to invalidate stale `ctx://` share-cache entries before awaiting `insetu:ensure-fresh-artifact` recompilation.
  * Delegated post-confirmation `SelectionStore` clearing to `<sutram-selection-tray>` / `<sutram-subtab-menu>` and declared `keepSelection: true` on dialog-handoff actions (`vfs-move`, `vfs-archive`, `vfs-delete`, `git-push`).
* **ExtensionRegistry Membrane Purification & Fitness/Unit Hardening (`insetu_sdk.js`, `config.js`, `rules_javascript.py`, `rules_python.py`, `js_loader.mjs`, `test_js_behavioral_units.py`):**
  * Removed monkey-patched `ExtensionRegistry` polyfills in `insetu_sdk.js` and migrated all callers in `insetu_element.js`, `ui_system_settings.js`, and `config.js` to public Sutram 2.11.0 registry methods (`getManifestEntries`, `getExtensionNames`, `getExtension`, `setActionFilter`, `getRepoConfigOptions`).
  * Tightened `PRIVATE_REGISTRY_ACCESS_BAN`, added `LEGACY_SHELL_HEADER_SLOT_BAN`, `PROCEDURAL_SELECTION_CLEAR_TIMER_BAN` and `DUPLICATE_REPO_FILTER_BAN` (Notes and Tracker exempt until Lane B's P20) in `rules_javascript.py`, set `MODULE_LINE_RATCHET` ceilings to real sizes in `rules_python.py` (listing `fs_store.js` at 802, owed back by the fs split), added `timer?.unref?.()` to `js_loader.mjs`, and added Sprint U3 unit assertions in `test_js_behavioral_units.py`.
